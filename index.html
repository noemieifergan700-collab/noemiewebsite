Research

Detecting Alzheimer's disease from resting-state EEG

M2 project, École Polytechnique · since October 2026

I compare two ways of reading EEG — a connectivity measure (phase lag index) and an entropy measure of how irregular the signal is — for telling 28 patients apart from 22 healthy controls, with SVMs and leave-one-out cross-validation. Entropy in the theta band separates the groups when brain regions are taken in pairs; connectivity doesn't. The best score (98% balanced accuracy) was picked out of 84 configurations, so I treat it as optimistic until it holds on independent data.

Brain connectivity after neurofeedback training

Research project, Télécom SudParis · with Rose Fakhrian and Kerman Arruartena, supervised by Nesma Houmani and Rita Yabouri

Using EEG from a double-blind neurofeedback trial on older adults, we turned 9,920 connectivity matrices into graphs and looked for changes after training: clustering, shortest paths, betweenness. Spectral biclustering picked out a group of five trained participants, but no method cleanly separated the trained and control groups.

Luminescent nanorods for mapping flow

Lab project, LPMC, École Polytechnique · supervised by Marcello Bonetti · in progress

I synthesize LaPO₄ nanorods doped with europium, or with erbium and ytterbium, and characterize them by electron microscopy, X-ray diffraction and photoluminescence. The group uses these rods as orientation probes: the polarization of their light shows how they line up in a flowing liquid, which gives a map of shear stress — including around living cells.
